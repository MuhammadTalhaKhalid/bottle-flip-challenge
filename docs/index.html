How to play
1. Tap Start camera and hold the phone low (near floor level).
2. Frame the bottle inside the dashed box — move closer/back & center it until it turns green and says READY. This matches how the AI was trained, which is what makes the judging accurate.
3. Pick a time (15/30/45/60s) and tap Start Challenge (unlocks at READY).
4. Flip! A good landing = bottle upright & still.
5. Flip as many as you can before the timer hits 0 — beat your high score 🏆

YOLOv8n bottle detection + MobileNetV3 temporal landing classifier (99.3% val accuracy), running entirely in your browser via ONNX Runtime Web. Nothing is uploaded.
